## 📌 Executive Summary
ZapFix is a civic utility digital public infrastructure (DPI) platform engineered to connect citizens with verified emergency technicians (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, gas) in under 15 minutes. 

Powered by **Google Gemini 1.5 Flash**, ZapFix acts as an on-demand **AI Safety Guardian**, delivering immediate, life-saving pre-arrival hazard isolation steps while technicians are en route.

---

## 🤖 Gemini 1.5 Flash AI Safety Engine (Prompt Configuration)

The core safety triage is powered by the following system instructions in Google AI Studio:

[SYSTEM INSTRUCTION]
You are the ZapFix AI Safety Guardian, an on-demand emergency triage engine operating in high-stress domestic and municipal crises.

[ROLE & OBJECTIVE]
When a citizen reports a critical utility failure (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Gas), your objective is to analyze the hazard and deliver immediate, life-saving pre-arrival isolation steps in under 850ms before the certified technician arrives on-site.

[SAFETY TRIAGE RULES]
1. ELECTRICAL: Immediate instructions to trip the main 40A double-pole circuit breaker at the distribution board. Warn against touching live water or exposed conductors.
2. PLUMBING: Immediate instructions to locate and shut the main brass quarter-turn water isolation valve to prevent structural flooding.
3. GAS / BOILER: Instruct immediate power cut, natural ventilation (open all exterior windows), and strictly prohibit open flames or electrical switches.
4. BREVITY: Keep instructions to 2-3 high-impact, bulleted imperative action steps. Zero fluff.

## 🚀 Key Features & Modules

1. **⚡ Instant Emergency Dispatch:** Emergency trade selection (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC) with rapid zero-delay workflow.
2. **🤖 Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Safety Guardian:** Context-aware emergency triage delivering life-saving containment protocols in under 850ms.
3. **🛡️ Worker Dignity & Access Safeguards:** Ephemeral residential gate passcode (auto-purged post-service) and Pet on Premises perimeter safety alert.
4. **💳 $75 Diagnostic Pre-Auth Escrow:** Transparent escrow hold preventing price gouging and protecting technicians against late cancellation.
5. **📍 Spatial Telemetry Engine:** Live GPS telemetry route tracking with 25-meter automated geofence triggering arrival PIN verification.
6. **✍️ Tamper-Evident Material Sign-Off:** Real-time on-site parts proposal, after-repair camera proof, and touch-screen electronic signature authorization.
7. **📑 Settled Tax Invoice (Cryptographic Audit):** Auto-generated invoice featuring dual verified GPS coordinates, SHA-256 before/after photo hashes, itemized parts, certified labor hours, and instant PDF export.
